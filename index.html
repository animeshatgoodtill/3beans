Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need Xero or QuickBooks already?

No. If you don't have one yet, we'll help you choose and set it up as part of onboarding. The subscription is in your name, so your data always belongs to you.

My POS is already set up. Is it too late?

Not at all. We can connect an existing POS at any time. If your books are behind, our catch-up service brings them up to date first.

How much of my time will this take?

A few minutes a week: snap your receipts, and update staff hours before each payroll. We handle the rest and only contact you when we need something.

Who checks my VAT return?

Every VAT return is prepared by your bookkeeper and reviewed by a qualified partner accountant before you approve it and we file it.

Can you do my year-end accounts too?

Yes. Our partner accountant prepares your year-end accounts and corporation tax return for £50 a month, so there's no big bill at year end.

Am I tied in?

Plans run for 12 months, then continue month to month with 30 days' notice.
