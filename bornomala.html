For grown-ups

How it works: letters come in small packs. Each pack has Meet → Trace → Read. Read only shows words built from letters already learned, so your child can genuinely read every sentence. Tap a word to hear it and see its letters. Tap “দেখি” to check with a picture.

Tracing: start at the green dot, follow the arrows to the star. Stroke order follows the NCTB Class 1 textbook (আমার বাংলা বই): top line (মাত্রা) first, left to right, then the letter body.

Buttons: every Bengali button has its English meaning underneath, and the helper voice says it when tapped.
